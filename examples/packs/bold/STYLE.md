# Sample bold style

Loud, fast and high-contrast: black and yellow, heavy type, nothing soft. For a creator whose videos feel like a
headline. Never pastel, never rounded, never slow.

## Type
- Display: Archivo Black, upright: hooks, thesis words, big figures
- Text: Archivo: captions, labels, text boxes
- Mono: IBM Plex Mono: every number, unit, kicker

## Colour
- Accent #FFD400: emphasis. Negative #FF4D4D: losses. Positive #3DDC84: gains.
- Grounds: light #FAFAFA, dark #0B0B0B. Flat, square corners.

## Captions
Black box, white text, uppercase, the emphasis word in yellow.

## Motion
Punchy: entrances in 0.2-0.3s, something new every 2-4 seconds, hard zooms on the key phrases.

## Sound
Dense effects. No music unless a track is handed over.

## Hi-fi
A one-line hook: one big display line, no eyebrow. Captions in short phrases, numbers set big, negations struck.
Words and figures behind the head often; a card every few lines. Hard zooms instead of settles; no black and white.
Cuts land with the voice (short J-cuts).

## Lo-fi
One word on screen at a time, uppercase, in the black box. Yellow text boxes for the hook and the CTA. Screenshots
slam in on top often; zooms on every line that lands.

## Showcase
A big word slams in whole. Whips, zooms, spins and drops on the cuts. Busy: an effect on every beat.

## Never
- Pastels, soft shadows, rounded corners, slow fades.
