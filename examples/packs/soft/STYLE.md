# Sample soft style

Warm, calm and friendly: cream paper, navy ink, a coral accent, an italic serif that feels handwritten. For a
creator whose videos feel like a conversation over coffee. Never harsh, never shouty.

## Type
- Display: Fraunces, italic: hooks, thesis words, big figures
- Text: DM Sans: captions, labels, text boxes
- Mono: DM Mono: every number, unit, kicker

## Colour
- Accent #F2765A: emphasis. Negative #E86A9A: losses. Positive #59B48C: gains.
- Grounds: light #FBF6EF, dark #1E2A4A. Graph paper, 12px corners.

## Captions
A navy box, sentence case, the emphasis word in coral.

## Motion
Normal: entrances in 0.3-0.4s, a new moment every 4-6 seconds, a small settle on every cut.

## Sound
Normal density. No music unless a track is handed over.

## Hi-fi
A two-line hook: a plain line over an italic display line. Captions in phrases, thesis words in the italic.
Cards often; things behind the head sometimes. Gentle settles and slow pushes; no black and white.

## Lo-fi
The whole caption line lands at once, three words at most. Cream text boxes for the hook and the CTA. Backdrops
behind the head often; no hard zooms.

## Showcase
A big word scales up from small. Pushes, reveals and drops on the cuts, never a whip. An effect on most beats.

## Never
- Neon, all caps, hard flashes, whip pans.
